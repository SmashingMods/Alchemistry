# Alchemistry 1.20.4-2.4.2 RELEASE

Requires ChemLib and AlchemyLib for this version.

Changes:
- Updated to Minecraft 1.20.4.
- JEI support is back: you can view and look up machine recipes (dissolver, combiner, and the rest) in the recipe viewer again.
- The Patchouli guidebook is back and loads correctly.
- Fixed an item-loss bug where the dissolver could drop leftover output when its buffer was full.
- Fixed a crash that could happen when breaking a reactor.
